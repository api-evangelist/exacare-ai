# ExaCare AI

> ExaCare AI is a post-acute and senior care operations platform that applies AI to admissions, referral management, prior authorization, and reimbursement workflows across skilled nursing, home health, hospice, and assisted living providers. It reads inbound referral and admissions packets, extracts clinical and payer data, and automates the back-office work of accepting and reimbursing patients. ExaCare is a private company backed by Insight Partners. It ships a customer web application and a Vanta-hosted Trust Center but publishes no public developer API at this time.
